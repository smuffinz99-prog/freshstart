    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Can't see yours? Email freshstartpressurewashing11@gmail.com and you'll get a real person.

    Will pressure washing damage my concrete or paint?

    Not the way we do it. Delicate surfaces like render, paint and timber get a low-pressure soft wash: a biodegradable solution does the heavy lifting, then we rinse gently. Real pressure is saved for tough surfaces like concrete and pavers.

    Do I need to be home?

    Nope. As long as we can reach the areas being cleaned and use an outside tap, you can be at work, on holiday or inside with a coffee. We'll send photos when we're done.

    Do you use my water?

    We connect to your outside tap. Because pressure cleaning works so much faster than a hose, it uses far less water to get the job done, and we always follow local water restrictions.

    Are your products safe for kids, pets and gardens?

    Yes. We use biodegradable, garden-safe detergents, wet down and rinse nearby plants, and just ask that pets stay inside while we work. Everything's safe to walk on once it's dry.

    How long does a job take?

    Most driveways take one to three hours. A single-storey house wash is usually half a day. You'll get a time estimate with your quote.

    What happens if it rains?

    Light rain doesn't stop us. If the weather turns properly bad, we'll move your booking at no cost.

    How often should I get things cleaned?

    Brisbane's humidity means mould and algae come back fast. Driveways and paths look their best with a clean every 12 to 18 months, and house washes every year or two.

    Are you insured?

    Yes. We carry full public liability insurance and we're happy to send a certificate of currency before we start.

    How do I pay?

    Once the job's done and you're happy, we send an invoice. Pay by card or bank transfer, or tap on the day.
