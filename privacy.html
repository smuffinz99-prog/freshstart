Privacy

Last updated November 2026

FreshStart Pressure Washing ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we look after it, in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you ask for a quote or book a job we collect your name, phone number, email address, suburb or street address, and details and photos of the areas you'd like cleaned.

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote, book and carry out the work, and send your invoice.
  • To contact you about your job, including reminders and follow-ups.
  • To ask, occasionally, for a review. You can say no at any time.

We never sell your information, and we don't send marketing unless you've asked for it.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the business, such as our email provider, form provider, accounting software and payment processor. They're only allowed to use it to provide those services to us.

Website analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits to this site. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you personally.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and only keep it for as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as required by law (for example, tax records).

Access, corrections and complaints

You can ask to see or correct the information we hold about you, or raise a privacy concern, by emailing freshstartpressurewashing11@gmail.com or calling 0491 570 156. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
